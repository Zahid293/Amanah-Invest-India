Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Amanah Invest India.

What is Amanah Invest India?

Amanah Invest India is a prototype platform designed to connect investors with real-asset development projects through a transparent digital interface.

How can I register?

You can create an account by visiting the Register page and completing the registration form.

How do I submit a project?

Project developers can use the "Submit Project" page to provide project information for review.

Are projects immediately available for investment?

No. Projects are intended to undergo review before being listed on the platform.

Is the platform operational?

This website is currently a prototype. The information displayed is for demonstration purposes while the platform is under development.
