KYC Verification

Know Your Customer (KYC) is an important process used to verify the identity of users and support a secure platform environment.

Why KYC Is Required

  • Verify user identity.
  • Improve platform security.
  • Support compliance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Help protect users from fraud and unauthorized activity.

Documents That May Be Requested

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Passport
  • Driving Licence
  • Voter ID
  • Proof of Address (where applicable)

Verification Process

  1. Create your account.
  2. Upload the requested documents (future feature).
  3. Our team reviews the submitted information.
  4. Your account status is updated after verification.
Prototype Notice

Document upload and online verification are not yet available. These features will be introduced in a future version of the platform.
